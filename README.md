# HR Attendance & Payroll SaaS Script

HRM Pro is HR, attendance, leave and payroll software sold as source code on Coderobotics. It is built with Laravel 12, Livewire 3, Alpine.js and Tailwind CSS v4 on MySQL/MariaDB. Buyers run it as their own SaaS: companies sign up, start a free trial, choose a monthly or yearly plan and pay by Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay or bank transfer, and each paid payment creates a numbered invoice with Indian GST support. Each company gets multi-branch HR with GPS and geofence clock-in, shifts, late/half-day/overtime rules, leave accrual and approvals, and payroll with PF, ESI, Professional Tax, LWF and TDS (old and new regime). It also covers payslip PDFs, HR letters, document expiry reminders, reports with charts, and six built-in roles plus custom roles. Employees can install it as a phone app (PWA) on Android and iPhone, with offline clock-in and push notifications. HRM Pro runs on ordinary shared hosting with PHP 8.2+ and one cron job, with no Node, Redis or WebSockets needed. It comes with a web installer, demo data and 200 automated tests. It suits entrepreneurs starting an HR SaaS business, agencies building HRMS for clients, and companies with several branches. The price is a one-time payment that includes the complete unencrypted source code, one year of free updates and one year of email support.

HRM Pro moves all of that into one online system, and you sell it to them. It’s a complete multi-company SaaS: companies sign up on your site, start a free trial, pick a plan and pay you online. Their HR team then runs attendance, leave and payroll in one place, and employees use it like a phone app.
